## 01. How To Write A Research Paper

**IMRaD structure:**

| Section | What's In It |
|---|---|
| **I**ntroduction | Why this matters |
| **M**ethods | How you did it |
| **R**esults | What you found |
| **a**nd | — |
| **D**iscussion | What it means |

**Writing rules:**
- Keep it simple
- One idea per paragraph
- Use figures and tables
- Cite other papers
- Proofread 3 times

**Length:** 8-12 pages for a research paper

---

## 02. Literature Review

**The job:** Show what others have done and what's missing.

**Steps:**
1. Search Google Scholar for keywords
2. Read 30-50 papers
3. Group by theme
4. Find the gap
5. Write 10-20 pages

**Tools:**
- Zotero (free reference manager)
- VOSviewer (for maps)

**Tip:** Read 1 paper per day. After 2 months, you'll have 60.
