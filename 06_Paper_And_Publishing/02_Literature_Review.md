# 02 — Literature Review

> **Show what others did. Find the gap. Win.**

---

## What Is A Literature Review?

A chapter in your thesis that:
1. Summarizes existing research
2. Compares different methods
3. Finds what's missing (the gap)
4. Justifies your work

**Length:** 15-25 pages in MSc thesis

---

## 5 Steps To Do It

### Step 1: Define Keywords
Example: `"machine learning" AND "diabetes" AND "prediction"`

### Step 2: Search Databases

| Database | Field |
|---|---|
| Google Scholar | All |
| IEEE Xplore | Engineering |
| PubMed | Medical |
| ACM | Computing |
| Scopus | Multidisciplinary |

### Step 3: Apply Filters
- Year (last 5 years preferred)
- Peer-reviewed
- English
- Relevant to your topic

### Step 4: Read Papers
- Use 3-pass method
- Take notes
- Group by theme

### Step 5: Write It
- Group by theme, not by author
- Use comparison tables
- End with "the gap"

---

## Organize Your Reading

### By Theme (Best)
```
Section 2.1: Method A
Section 2.2: Method B
Section 2.3: Method C
Section 2.4: The Gap
```

### By Time
- Old → New (shows evolution)

### Comparison Table Example

| Paper | Year | Method | Dataset | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Smith | 2022 | SVM | Pima | 78% |
| Lee | 2023 | RF | Pima | 82% |
| Park | 2024 | XGBoost | Pima | 85% |
