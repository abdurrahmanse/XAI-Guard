## Conference vs Journal

| | Conference | Journal |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | 3-6 months | 6-18 months |
| Papers/year | Many | Few |
| Acceptance | 20-30% | 20-40% |
| Audience | Researchers | Researchers |
| Best for | New work | Detailed work |

## For Your MSc Thesis

Don't aim for NeurIPS on day one. Try:

1. **arXiv** (free, instant) — always submit here first
2. **Workshop papers** (easier acceptance)
3. **Local conferences** in your country
4. **Student conferences**
5. **Then try a top venue** if results are good
