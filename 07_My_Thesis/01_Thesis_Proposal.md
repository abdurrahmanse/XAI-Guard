## 📋 Thesis Structure

**6 chapters:**

1. **Introduction** (10-15 pages)
   - Why this matters
   - Research questions
   - Your contribution

2. **Literature Review** (15-25 pages)
   - What others did
   - What's missing
   - How your work fills the gap

3. **Methodology** (15-20 pages)
   - Data
   - Methods
   - Setup

4. **Results** (15-20 pages)
   - What you found
   - Tables & figures
   - Comparisons

5. **Discussion** (10-15 pages)
   - What it means
   - Limitations
   - Future work

6. **Conclusion** (3-5 pages)
   - Summary
   - Main contribution

**Total: ~70-100 pages**

- Practice 5+ times
- Anticipate 20 questions
- Know your limitations
- Be confident, not arrogant
- Smile 😊
