---

## Writing Tips

- **Don't aim for perfect.** Get words on paper first.
- **Write every day.** Even 200 words counts.
- **Show your draft to your advisor early.** Don't hide it.
- **Revise later.** First draft is just to start.
- **Use references.** Cite as you go.
- **Use version control.** Commit your draft to Git.

---

## Typical Chapter Sizes

| Chapter | Pages |
|---|---|
| Introduction | 10-15 |
| Literature Review | 15-25 |
| Methodology | 15-20 |
| Results | 15-20 |
| Discussion | 10-15 |
| Conclusion | 3-5 |
| **Total** | **70-100** |

---

## When You're Stuck

- Talk to your advisor
- Read more papers in your area
- Take a walk
- Write about something else
- Don't give up
